# 🐎 All The Pretty Stallions — Android app

Install the **Pretty Stallions** Android app (the run-club leaderboard / LSS 2026).

## 📲 Install (scan the QR)

<img src="qr.png" width="260" alt="Scan to install">

…or tap this link on your phone:
**[⬇️ Download PrettyStallions.apk](https://github.com/fabfreebird23/pretty-stallions-app/releases/latest/download/PrettyStallions.apk)**

### Steps
1. On your Android phone, scan the QR (or open the download link).
2. Tap the downloaded **PrettyStallions.apk**.
3. Android will ask to **allow installing unknown apps** from your browser — allow it, then tap **Install**.
4. Open **Pretty Stallions** from your app drawer. 🐎

> iPhone? Sorry — this is Android only. On iOS, just open the site in Safari and
> tap Share → **Add to Home Screen** for an app-like icon.

## What it is
A lightweight wrapper around the live leaderboard site, so it lives as a real app
on your phone (own icon, fullscreen). It needs an internet connection.

Works on Android 7.0 and newer.
